2. Business Problem Statement

The studio does visible, skilled Indian-style makeover work, but has no repeatable customer engine. Visibility depends on walk-ins and word of mouth. There is no pipeline: no lead capture, no booking cadence, no follow-up, no package offer. Growth depends on who walks in, not on a designed funnel.

The fix is a functioning local engine: discoverable location, qualified leads, predictable bookings, repeat customers.

3. Positioning

“Aaradhya Make Over n Art offers Indian-style makeover and bridal styling done with a personal, home-town touch, in Sangrur.”
  1. “Indian-style” differentiates against generic west-style parlours in the same market.
  2. “Home-town touch” fights price-only comparison with big-city brands.
  3. Local trust beats distant influencers for bridal bookings.

Keep the tagline identical on GMB, Instagram bio, WhatsApp status and flyers. Consistency builds the brand.

5. Google Business Profile: The Quick Win

Single highest ROI action in the first 30 days. Local makeover searches are decided on the mobile GMB card.

  1. Category set to Beauty salon / Make-up artist.
  2. Confirm exact address + pin, phone, working hours.
  3. Add 10+ photos: interiors, before/after, bridal work, shelf.
  4. Post 2 Google posts per week (offer + service + before/after).
  5. Ask for a Google review at checkout. Target 10 in month 1, then 5 per month.
  6. Reply to every review within 48 hours.
  7. Enable the WhatsApp button so “get quote” lands in DMs.

KPI: GMB reviews 0 to 10+ in 30 days; photos 0 to 10+.

Design target: every enquiry becomes a booked slot in under 24 hours.

Track per week: enquiries, booked, advances, no-shows, rebooks. Five numbers on a sheet. If conversion sits under 60%, tighten the qualify step.

8. Retention & Repeat Business

  1. Membership card: flat member price on 3+ visits per month.
  2. Off-peak days (Tue / Thu): 15% off to lift empty slots.
  3. Seasonal push: bridal season (Oct to Feb) opens early-date packages.
  4. Birthday baseline: client card notes birthday, sends an offer that week.

Repeat rule of thumb: 30% of monthly revenue from repeat clients within 90 days, or the retention loop gets fixed first.

9. Assumptions to Confirm

  1. Actual monthly bookings and average ticket.
  2. Worker count (owner alone vs. team of 2-3).
  3. Monthly marketing budget (suggested starting range: 3,000-8,000 INR).
  4. Who answers DMs today and response time.
  5. Existing review counts on GMB + Instagram.

If an assumption is wrong, change that row, not the system.

11. 90-Day Roadmap

Phase A (Weeks 1-2): Foundation. GMB cleanup + WhatsApp button; finalize menu and prices everywhere; Instagram profile cleanup (name, bio, category, WhatsApp link).

Phase B (Weeks 3-6): Engine. Review drive to 10; content cadence live (4 posts + 3 reels/week); DM reply templates built; first tracker sheet filled.

Phase C (Weeks 7-12): Repeat & Ads. Membership + referral offers live; first paid push on proven reels only; review the 5-number sheet and adjust qualify; add the studio walkthrough video when footage is ready.

Review gate, week 12: if reviews, conversion and reels output are on plan, extend to the full 360-degree scope with a second proposal.

12. What Needs the Owner

  1. Real numbers every week (bookings, ticket, reviews).
  2. Photo and video permission for marketing.
  3. Fast DM replies, or one designated phone.
  4. Approval to run small paid pushes on proven reels.

The studio will not grow from posting. It grows from a repeatable local engine: discover, qualify, book, follow up, rebook. This document is the engine blueprint.
