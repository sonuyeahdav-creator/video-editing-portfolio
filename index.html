A little about me

Editor by craft. Storyteller by habit.

I started in journalism and marketing - and somewhere between covering stories and running campaigns, I fell hard for the edit. Since then I've cut corporate films, property showcases, podcast episodes and social reels for teams like RivaKenko Pharma and community work with Pehchaan The Street School.

I don't just splice clips together. I care about pacing, sound, color and the little emotional moments that make a video feel alive - and I finish every piece looking like it belongs to the brand, not the timeline.
